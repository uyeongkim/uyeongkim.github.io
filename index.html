Hi! I am a M.S. candidate at the KAIST Graduate School of AI, advised by Kijung Shin at the Data Mining Lab since Fall 2023. I am also a Research Engineer at NC AI. Before that, I was a SW engineering intern at GraphAI. My research interests include large language models, graph representation learning, multi-agent systems, and embodied AI.
